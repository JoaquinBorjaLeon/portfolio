Every day it watches a list of stocks, scores each company on its fundamentals and flags anything worth a second look. It analyses and watches; it never predicts or trades.

What it does

  • Downloads daily prices and annual statements into SQLite. Missing data is shown as “n/a”: it is never invented.
  • Scores each company from 0 to 100 on eight criteria and lists red flags separately.
  • Alerts when a stock enters its zone or gets within 3%, crosses its 200-day moving average or gets a new red flag.
  • Every zone alert includes the latest headlines and my own note on the stock.

How it is built

  • Weights and thresholds in YAML, to tune the method without touching code.
  • An anti-spam table stops the same alert repeating for 5 days.
  • JSON output for an n8n workflow, or direct delivery through the Telegram API.
  • A FastAPI panel with no write endpoints and a demo mode with synthetic data.
