My part

  • Project manager of a 15-person team: coordination, sprint planning and weekly progress pitches.
  • Integration: I reviewed and merged more than 80 pull requests and prepared the release for every milestone.
  • Continuous integration: lint workflows for backend and frontend and commit message validation.
  • Security: static analysis (SAST), dependency scanning with Dependabot and an automated monthly security review.
  • Dependencies pinned with pip-compile, plus a guide so the whole team handled them the same way.

What the app does

  • Incident reports with status tracking.
  • Shared-space bookings with a calendar and a QR pass.
  • Votes weighted by property share.
  • An assistant that answers from the community rules and documents.
  • Minutes summarised from the meeting recording, and chat between residents.
