RecuerdaMed

A cross-platform app that helps older people on several medications not to miss a dose, and alerts caregivers and family when one is missed.

What it does

  • Each medication stores dose, form, notes and an optional reference photo, taken with the camera or from the gallery.
  • Schedules can be daily, on set days of the week or every X hours. Each dose moves through scheduled, pending, confirmed or expired according to its time window.
  • Local reminder, warning and expiry notifications, plus push alerts to linked users.
  • Three roles: patients invite caregivers and family, decide who sees their doses and can revoke access at any time.
  • An adherence history of confirmed and expired doses.

How it is built

  • React Native with Expo and TypeScript: one codebase for Android, iOS and web.
  • Firebase with access control enforced in the database security rules and 11 composite indexes.
  • Business logic split by entity, with pure functions covered by 18 unit tests.
  • Continuous integration with GitHub Actions and web deployment on Vercel.
