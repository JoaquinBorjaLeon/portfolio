DocuChat

An assistant that answers questions about your PDFs with retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) and shows which document each answer comes from. If something is not in your documents, it says so.

What it does

  • Splits your PDFs, embeds them with nomic-embed-text and stores them in PostgreSQL with pgvector.
  • You ask in plain language and every answer cites its source documents.
  • It runs fully locally with Ollama, or with Gemini in the cloud, chosen with an environment variable.

How it is built

  • Hybrid search. Vector search failed on short questions like “What is the PMBOK?”: the right passage did not even make the top-k. Combining it with PostgreSQL full-text search and fusing both with Reciprocal Rank Fusion moved that passage to first place.
  • Tool-calling agent. For each question the model decides whether to search the documents or check the date. I compared Ollama and Gemini: both choose well; Ollama is free and private, Gemini polishes answers a little more.
  • Observability. Every query stores provider, tools, retrieved passages and latency, so I can measure instead of guessing.
