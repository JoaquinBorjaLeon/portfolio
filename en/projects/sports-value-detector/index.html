Sports Value Detector

It prices football and tennis markets with its own models and compares them with the closing odds of sharp bookmakers, the toughest bar there is. No model beats it consistently, and the project says so plainly.

What it does

  • Gathers 5 football leagues over 5 seasons (about 8,900 matches) and about 13,000 ATP matches, joining two sources with a 99.8% match rate.
  • Computes probabilities with its own models and compares them with the closing odds.
  • Logs odds for markets with no free history, such as corners and cards, to build its own.
  • A FastAPI and React dashboard to explore matches, backtests and calibration.

How it is built

  • No leakage. Features are computed in a single chronological pass, and tests truncate and perturb the future to check that the past does not change.
  • Temporal validation on two separate held-out seasons, so no model is picked by luck.
  • Models: logistic regression for 1X2, Dixon-Coles for goals and surface-specific Elo with logistic regression for tennis.
  • Sceptical of wins: a backtest with a +1.0% ROI turned out to be noise (+0.24 sigma) and was discarded.
