Salon booking and scheduling

A web app for a hair salon: clients book a service, a stylist and a time in three steps, and the business runs its calendar from an admin panel.

My part

  • The whole frontend with React, Vite and Tailwind: public site, authentication, booking wizard, “My appointments” and the full admin panel.
  • State and data with Zustand and TanStack Query, and the admin calendar with FullCalendar.
  • In the API: endpoints for appointments, stylists, services, time blocks, notes and settings, with a generic validation middleware, cached settings and timezone-safe dates.
  • Also: breaks, time blocks, the mobile view and deployment.

What the app does

  • A guided three-step booking: service, a time from the actual free slots, and confirmation.
  • A client area with upcoming appointments, history and cancellation.
  • A business panel with the day’s summary, calendar, services, staff, time blocks and settings.
