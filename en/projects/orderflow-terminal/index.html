Orderflow Terminal

A personal terminal to study the options market and company financials in one place: flow and gamma exposure from the full chain, price structure, fundamentals read straight from the SEC and an AI summary whose every number is checked against the data.

What it does

  • Flow: the stock chart with gamma levels (call wall, put wall and zero gamma), supply and demand zones, structure breaks and Fibonacci, next to the options chain and the net call and put premium.
  • Gamma exposure by strike and expiry, with a heatmap that compares each level with the day’s first snapshot.
  • Quarterly fundamentals from the SEC, a bear, base and bull valuation from a DCF and the company’s own historical multiples, and an earnings simulator.
  • On-demand AI analysis that sums up gamma, structure, technicals and consensus, and checks that every number in the text exists in the app’s data.
  • Market overview: sector map, top movers, market regime and SEC catalysts.

How it is built

  • A TypeScript pnpm monorepo: a Fastify and uWebSockets.js gateway and a Next.js 15 and React 19 web app that share types and Zod schemas.
  • Provider-agnostic ingestion: free CBOE data by default, or Interactive Brokers and Schwab for real-time data, with detection of stalled connections and automatic reconnection.
  • Under load, quotes are dropped before trades, and charts render on canvas at 60 fps by batching ticks per frame.
  • No made-up data: if a source fails, the UI says so; a missing value is shown as a dash, never as zero, and every figure keeps its provenance.
  • Deployed with Docker on my server and reachable only from my private Tailscale network.
